# Apogix

Multi-tenant social publishing & scheduling SaaS.
Create once. Adapt intentionally. Publish everywhere from one calm workspace.

Pick a date, pick the channels, and Apogix publishes your post to every platform you chose — server-side, whether your browser is open or not.

## Homepage
`marketing/index.html` — standalone marketing homepage (open in a browser). Ports into `apps/web` later.
