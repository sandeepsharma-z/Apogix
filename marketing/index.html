Scheduling that holds

Set the date. Close the laptop. It still goes out.

Tap any date on the calendar, set the time, and choose the channels for that post. Apogix queues one job per channel, so a hiccup on one platform never blocks the others.

  • Any date and time — schedule days or months ahead, down to the minute.
  • Per-post channel choice — different posts, different platforms, no lock-in.
  • Drag to reschedule — move a post on the calendar; the timezone is always shown.
  • Runs on our servers — publishing never depends on a browser tab staying open.
  • Retries without duplicates — a transient failure is retried; it never double-posts.
One post, many shapes

The same idea, tuned for each channel.

Your master caption stays the source of truth. Any channel that needs something different gets its own version — and Apogix checks each one against that platform's real limits before it's queued.

  • Character and media checks per platform, up front.
  • First comment support where the platform allows it.
  • Non-destructive crops — your original file is always kept.
  • Previews are indicative — we're honest that native rendering can vary.

Built to not miss

When something breaks, you get an action — not a mystery.

  • One job per destination — Instagram failing doesn't hold up LinkedIn.
  • Idempotent retries — transient errors retry with backoff; permission errors don't.
  • Honest partial success — never a green checkmark when only two of three went out.
  • Reconnect alerts — we warn you before an expiring token affects a scheduled post.
  • Plain-language errors — each one names the platform and the next step.
  • Provider outages handled — we pause and back off instead of failing you instantly.

Questions

The things people ask first.

01Which platforms are supported?+

Instagram Professional, Facebook Pages and LinkedIn at launch. TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest and X are on the roadmap.

02Do I have to give Apogix my passwords?+

No. You connect through each platform's official login screen. Apogix never sees or stores your social password, and your access tokens never touch the browser.

03What happens if a post fails?+

We retry the failures that can be retried, leave the ones that can't alone, and send you a plain-language notification with the platform name and what to do next.

04Does scheduling work if my computer is off?+

Yes. Publishing runs on Apogix servers on a queue, not in your browser. Schedule a post and close everything — it still goes out at the time you set.

05Can my team approve posts before they publish?+

On Professional and Agency plans. Editors submit a version, approvers accept or request changes, and every transition is recorded in the audit log.

06How am I billed?+

Monthly in Indian rupees through Razorpay. Cancel anytime — your access runs to the end of the billing period, and your invoices stay visible afterward.
